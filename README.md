## About

I am a master’s student in Statistics, Analytics, and Data Science at
UNC–Chapel Hill. My interests lie at the intersection of statistical
methodology, evaluation of interactive AI systems, agent-based simulation,
and human decision-making.

I build research systems that emphasize transparent assumptions, auditable
evaluation, and reproducible computational experiments.

## Current Research

- **Poker decision support and skill development** — an ongoing collaboration
  with UNC faculty investigating how analytical and AI-assisted tools may help
  people improve decision-making skills.

- **Robust-reference divergence profiles** — independent methodological
  research on statistical sensitivity analysis, experimental validation, and
  reproducible manuscript development.
